Privacy Policy for Bobik catches donuts

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bobik catches donuts (the "Game"), developed by KqirillCorporation (the "Developer"), handles user information.

1. Data Collection

The Game does not collect, store or share any personal data. The Game does not request your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress (completed levels, high scores, donuts and boosters) and settings (sound volume) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the Developer or any third party. Uninstalling the Game removes this data from your device.

3. Ads and Purchases

The Game contains no advertising and no real-money purchases. In-game currency can only be earned by playing.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game is built with the Unity engine. The Game does not use analytics services, ad networks or any other third-party services to collect data. Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Children

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. Since the Game does not collect any personal data, it does not collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always available on this page. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
